About

Grounded in real service work, built on formal marketing training.

Cordeiro Creatives was founded by Chase Cordeiro, a Scituate, Massachusetts native with a BS in Marketing. Before starting the company, Chase spent his college years working as a scuba diver and in construction — jobs that put him face-to-face with dozens of service businesses. What he noticed was simple: the ones with a strong social media presence pulled ahead, and the ones without it struggled to compete.

That gap is what Cordeiro Creatives exists to close. Combining formal marketing training with real, on-the-ground experience in service industries, Chase helps businesses build the kind of content and online presence that turns visibility into growth — through drone photography and video, general content creation, and hands-on marketing consulting.

But the goal isn't to make clients dependent. Cordeiro Creatives works alongside business owners every step of the way, teaching them to create their own content so they can eventually stand on their own — with Chase as their coach, not just their contractor.
